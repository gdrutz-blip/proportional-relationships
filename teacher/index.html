How to use it

  • Early finishers: send students to the game for today’s lesson. A round takes about 5 to 10 minutes.
  • At home: post the link in Google Classroom with the lesson’s game named, for example “Play Price Board after Lesson 5.”
  • Review days: students pick the games for the lessons they found hardest, then try the Festival Weekend boss. It mixes every tool, like the Boss question on the test.

How each game works

  • Each round has 6 questions (Percent Builder has 7, and the boss has 5 multi-step problems). The numbers are new every time and kept friendly for no-calculator work, like the unit test.
  • After one wrong try, students get a hint that uses the lesson’s key move: a double number line, a ratio table, a bar model, benchmarks or the value for 1. Many common mistakes get their own feedback, for example dividing by the new amount, 13% as 13 cents, or 18 months used as 18 years.
  • After a second wrong try, the game shows the worked solution. Students can then press Try a similar one for the same kind of question with new numbers. Practice questions don’t change their points.
  • Scoring: 2 points for a first-try answer, 1 for a second try. A finished round earns 1 to 3 stars, and the game’s spot on the truck gets a star.
  • There are no timers. Nothing flashes.
  • Equivalent answers count: 1.2 or 1.20, $63.28 or 63.28, 25% or 0.25. Money answers like 1.2 also get a reminder to write $1.20.

Matches the Unit 3 materials

  • The key moves, hints and traps use the wording from the Toolkit, wall chart and Day 2 handouts.
  • Zuri’s recipes are the unit’s: Berry Blast 2:3, Mango Rush 4:6, Green Machine 3:5, Tropical Twist 3:2, Peach Fizz 5:2.
  • Ontario HST is 13% (× 1.13). Tips are on the food price before tax, as in the Lesson 6 handout. Money is rounded only at the final step.

Privacy

  • No logins, no accounts and no names. Nothing students type is sent anywhere. The site is set up to block it from contacting any server.
  • Stars are saved only in the browser on that device. On a shared Chromebook, use Reset my stars at the bottom of the home screen.
  • Because stars stay on the device, the site can’t show you student results. Ask students to show you their stars, or use your exit tickets.
